# RepairTrack privacy and retention policy

## Purpose and consent

RepairTrack stores customer contact information, device details, reported faults, repair photographs, quotations, invoices and payment records only to manage repair services. Before creating a customer record, reception must explain this purpose and record the customer's consent using the required checkbox.

Customers must not be asked for passwords, financial PINs or unrelated personal information. Repair photographs must avoid unrelated people, identity documents, private messages and personal screen content.

## Access

- Administrators manage the system and assignments.
- Receptionists access customer, repair, quotation, invoice and counter-payment records.
- Technicians access only repairs assigned to them and may upload relevant repair images.
- Customers access a repair using the repair number and registered phone number.

Staff accounts are individual and must not be shared. Access is logged where the system provides an audit event.

## Recommended retention schedule

- Active repair records and photographs: for the duration of the repair.
- Completed repair records, quotations and photographs: 24 months for warranty and service history, unless local law requires otherwise.
- Invoices and payment records: retain for the legally required accounting period; confirm the Sri Lankan requirement with a qualified accountant or lawyer.
- Password-reset codes: 10 minutes; expired records should be deleted by the cleanup task.
- Application logs: 30 to 90 days, excluding passwords, tokens and verification codes.
- Backups: daily backups for 30 days, encrypted and access-controlled.

## Customer requests and incidents

The repair centre must define a contact for access, correction and deletion requests. Legal/accounting records may need to be retained even after a deletion request. Suspected unauthorised access must be documented, contained, investigated and reported as required by applicable law.

This document is an operational template, not legal advice. Review it against Sri Lankan data-protection and accounting requirements before production use.
